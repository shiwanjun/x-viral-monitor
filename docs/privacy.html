✦ X-Tools

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

X-Tools is a browser extension for X (Twitter). It does not sell personal data and does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels.

What X-Tools accesses

  • X pages and X API responses: on x.com and pro.x.com, to read tweet metrics and render the features you enable. Processing happens in your browser.
  • Your extension settings: preferences such as thresholds, display choices, prompt templates, and feature toggles are stored with Chrome storage. Some settings may sync through your Google account when Chrome Sync is enabled.
  • Local data library: bookmarks, likes, your original posts, your replies, tags, folders, and source-deletion state are normalized and stored in extension IndexedDB. Data is isolated by your numeric X account ID.
  • Optional Pro cloud backup: only after explicit consent, normalized post metadata, original media URLs, tags, folders, and deletion state are encrypted in transit and stored by X-Tools for cross-device recovery. X cookies, bearer tokens, raw GraphQL responses, AI keys, and media files are never uploaded. Cloud backup becomes read-only when Pro expires and is deleted after a 30-day retention period.
  • Optional external services: when you configure and use an AI provider, the prompt and relevant tweet text are sent directly from your browser to that provider. AI keys remain in the extension. Membership activation and validation contact the X-Tools service. Filter-rule updates may be downloaded from GitHub.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use browsing history for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not collect login credentials or passwords.
  • We do not upload X cookies, bearer tokens, raw GraphQL responses, AI keys, or media files.
  • We do not inject advertisements.

Your choices

You can disable optional features and cloud backup at any time. The data center provides local archive, cloud-backup deletion, and export controls. Removing the extension removes its local IndexedDB data. Data sent to an AI provider you choose is governed by that provider's policy.
